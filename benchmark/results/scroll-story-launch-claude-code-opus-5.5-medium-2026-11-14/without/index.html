Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

How does Tidal decide my paycheck?

It looks at what you've earned over the last twelve months, takes out your tax set-aside, keeps a small slice to grow your buffer, and divides the rest by twelve. It recalculates every quarter and only moves the number gradually, so you never get whiplash.

What happens if I hit a really long dry spell?

Your paycheck keeps coming from the pool. If the pool would run low within the next few months, Tidal tells you early and suggests a gentle step down — you approve any change before it happens.

Can I get at my money whenever I want?

Yes. The pool is your money. Withdraw any amount at any time, or pay yourself a one-off bonus. Tidal just recalculates your runway afterwards.

Is Tidal a bank?

No. Tidal is a money app. Your pool and tax jar are held in accounts at partner banks, and your paycheck goes to whichever everyday account you choose.

Is the tax jar tax advice?

It's a smart savings habit, not advice. Tidal suggests a rate from what you tell us, and you can change it anytime. For anything complicated, your accountant is still your best friend.
