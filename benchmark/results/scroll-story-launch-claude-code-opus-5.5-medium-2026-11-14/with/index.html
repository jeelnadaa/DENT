The tide table for freelance income Waitlist edition · November 2026

Your income comes in waves. Your paycheck doesn’t have to.

Tidal is a buffer account for freelancers. Client payments land in your buffer whenever they arrive. Tidal pays you the same amount, on the same day, every month.

01 — The story

One year of freelance pay, smoothed.

Scroll through an example year and watch each client payment pour into the buffer and come back out as the same paycheck, month after month.

Example year

01 Swell 02 Worry 03 Harbour 04 Tide 05 Steady

  1. 01 · The swell

    Freelance money arrives like weather.

    Here’s an example freelancer’s year, April to March. ₹2,40,000 lands in April. June, August and November bring nothing at all.

    Example data. Year total ₹9,40,000, an average of ₹78,333 a month.

  2. 02 · The worry

    Bills arrive like clockwork.

    Say rent, EMIs and groceries cost ₹75,000 a month. In 7 of these 12 months, client payments fall short of that line.

    The year is fine on average. The months aren’t.

  3. 03 · The harbour

    Every payment lands in your buffer first.

    With Tidal, client payments go into your buffer, not straight into your spending money. April’s ₹2,40,000 fills it.

    Then, on payday, Tidal pays you ₹75,000. The rest stays in the harbour.

  4. 04 · The tide

    Big months fill it. Quiet months draw on it.

    Month after month, the same motion: invoices raise the buffer, dry spells lower it, and payday never moves. ₹75,000, twelve times.

  5. 05 · Steady

    Same year. Same clients. Twelve identical paydays.

    The same ₹9,40,000 came in. But no month fell short, and ₹40,000 was still in the buffer at year end. The lowest it ever got was ₹10,000, in February.

    In this example the paycheck sits a little under the ₹78,333 monthly average. That gap is what keeps a cushion in the buffer.

02 — How it works

Three steps, then it runs on its own.

  1. 01

    Point client payments to your buffer

    Put your Tidal account details on your invoices. Every payment, big or small, lands in your buffer.

  2. 02

    Choose your monthly paycheck

    Pick an amount your buffer can keep up with. Not sure what that is? Try your own year below.

  3. 03

    Get paid the same day, every month

    Payday comes on the same date for the same amount, whether a client paid this month or not.

05 — Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Can I use Tidal yet?

Not yet. Tidal is opening to the waitlist first. Join with your email and we’ll tell you when you can sign up. [Add launch date when known.]

How is this different from a savings account?

A savings account holds money until you move it. Tidal also pays it back to you on a schedule: the same amount, on the same day, every month. A big invoice turns into several months of steady pay instead of one good month.

What happens in a month when no client pays?

Your paycheck is paid from your buffer, so it still arrives as long as the buffer holds enough. That’s what the June, August and November dips in the story show.

What if my buffer runs low?

Tidal pays you from your own money in the buffer. It isn’t a loan, so the paycheck needs to be an amount your buffer can sustain. The “Try your year” tool shows how to pick one.
