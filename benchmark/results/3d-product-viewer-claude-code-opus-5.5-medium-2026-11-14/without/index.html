Four modules, one click

Each joint uses the same magnetic bayonet. Twist to lock, twist to release. No tools, no screws to lose.

01

Weighted base

2.1 kg cast steel with a cork underside. Holds the long arm without tipping. Clamp and wall mounts also fit.

02

Twin-rod arm

Friction hinges hold any angle. Three lengths, and the cable runs hidden inside the rods.

03

Interchangeable head

A wide dome for reading, a narrow cone for detail work. Both turn 300°.

04

Light engine

8 W LED, CRI 97, 2700–4000 K. Touch dimming on the head, and the engine lifts out for replacement.
