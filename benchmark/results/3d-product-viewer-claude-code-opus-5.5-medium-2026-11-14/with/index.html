Built from five parts.

Every joint is the same 4 mm bolt and friction knob. If one part breaks or you want a new finish, swap that part and keep the rest.

  1. 01

    Base

    Weighted cast-iron disc with a rubber foot. Holds the dimmer and the cable exit.

  2. 02

    Riser

    Swivel turret and shoulder joint. Turns the whole arm around the base.

  3. 03

    Lower arm

    Twin steel rods with a balance spring, so the arm holds any angle.

  4. 04

    Upper arm

    Second rod pair and spring, with the elbow joint the cable loops around.

  5. 05

    Head

    Spun shade with a recessed diffuser. The LED and its driver live here.

Questions

What do I need to put it together?

Only the 4 mm hex key in the box. Each module bolts to the next through its joint, and the cable clips along the arms.

Will the head stay where I leave it?

Yes. Two balance springs carry the weight of the arms, and the friction knob on each joint sets how stiff it feels.

What happens if the LED fails?

The LED and its driver live in the head. Replace the head module and keep the base and arms.

Can I change the finish later?

Each module comes in all three finishes, so you can swap a single part or all five.

Why do I see a “Lite” viewer instead of 3D?

On older phones, with data saver on, or in browsers without WebGL, the page shows a lighter turntable made of still images so it stays fast. You can still turn the lamp, change the finish and switch the light on. Where 3D is possible, the “3D” button above the viewer loads it.
