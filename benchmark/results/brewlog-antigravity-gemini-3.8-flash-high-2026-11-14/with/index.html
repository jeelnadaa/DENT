Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Brewlog, hardware compatibility, and Pro billing.

Brewlog is hardware-agnostic. You only need whatever you brew with today! Whether you use a simple plastic V60, an AeroPress, a Chemex, a French press, or a dual-boiler espresso machine, you can log your variables and dial in repeatable cups. A kitchen scale with gram precision is recommended.

As you rate cups, Brewlog’s correlation engine cross-references your sensory ratings against your grind clicks, water temperature, ratio, and bean roast age. When you log multiple brews with the same bag, Brewlog identifies the golden extraction plateau and tells you whether grinding finer or tweaking water temperature by 1°C will elevate sweetness.
