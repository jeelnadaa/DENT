No. 02 How it works

Three habits, one better cup.

You already change one thing at a time when you brew. Brewlog writes it down, so the lessons stick.

  1. 1

    Log the recipe

    Pick your beans, then note grind size, water temperature, ratio and brew time. A running timer fills in the time for you.

  2. 2

    Rate the cup

    Give it one to five stars and tap the flavours you notice. Add a note in your own words if you like.

  3. 3

    Find your best settings

    Brewlog lines up your ratings against your settings, so you can see what your favourite cups have in common.

No. 06 Questions

Before you start brewing.

Still unsure? The free plan is the quickest way to find out if logging suits you.

Do I need a scale or a thermometer?

No. Log whatever you know, even "kettle just off the boil" or "two scoops". The more precisely you measure, the clearer your patterns get, so many brewers add a scale later.

Which brew methods does Brewlog work with?

Any of them: pour-over, French press, AeroPress, moka pot, espresso, cold brew. Every method uses the same fields: beans, grind size, water temperature, ratio and time.

How does Brewlog know which settings are best?

It compares your ratings with the settings you logged. When your highest-rated cups share a temperature, ratio or grind, Brewlog points it out. It learns from your taste, not from anyone else's.

Patterns get clearer as you log more brews of the same beans.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free covers the core habit: log brews, rate cups and keep a limited number of saved recipes. Pro adds unlimited recipes, bean inventory tracking and brew analytics.

How much is Pro, and what's the yearly saving?

Pro is $6 a month, or $60 a year. Twelve months at $6 would be $72, so paying yearly saves $12, the same as getting two months free.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Everything you log on the free plan carries over when you upgrade to Pro.
