FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Write to hello@brewlog.example and a real person (who also drinks too much coffee) will reply.

Do I need special equipment to use Brewlog?

No. Brewlog works with any method: pour-over, AeroPress, French press, espresso, moka pot or cold brew. A kitchen scale helps you log accurate doses, but you can also log by scoops and cups.

How does Brewlog figure out my best settings?

Each time you rate a cup, Brewlog connects that score to the recipe behind it. Once you have a handful of brews for a bean or method, analytics show which grind, temperature, ratio and time ranges line up with your highest ratings.

The more you log, the sharper the picture gets. Most people see clear patterns after 10–15 brews.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you save up to 15 recipes and log unlimited brews with ratings and notes. Pro adds unlimited recipes, bean inventory tracking and brew analytics. Pro costs $6 a month or $60 a year.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. Every account can try Pro free for 14 days. We'll remind you three days before the trial ends, and if you don't upgrade you simply move back to Free. Nothing you logged is lost.

Can I cancel or switch plans any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in two taps. Monthly plans stop at the end of the billing period; yearly plans can be refunded pro-rata within the first 30 days.

Is my brew data private? Can I export it?

Your log is private by default and never sold. You can export everything to CSV at any time, and deleting your account deletes your data.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The mobile apps let you log and time brews without a connection and sync automatically when you're back online.
