# S27 explainer narration, take 2 (take 1 ran 73 s and had two TTS artefacts). One line per caption-sized thought.
A password is a secret you hand over. To every site, every time.
Sites keep copies. Fake sites ask just as nicely.
A passkey isn't a secret you share. It's a signature only your device can make.
Your device makes a pair of keys. The private key never leaves it, and only your face, fingerprint or PIN unlocks it.
The site keeps only the public key. It can check your signature, but never forge one.
To sign in, the site sends a fresh challenge. Your device signs it, and the site checks.
No secret crosses the wire. A stolen public key signs no one in.
Each passkey is bound to the real site's domain. A lookalike gets nothing.
Your keychain or password manager syncs them across your devices.
Stop handing over secrets. Look for "Create a passkey" in your settings.
